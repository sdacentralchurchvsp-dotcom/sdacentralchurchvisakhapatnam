A Warm Welcome from Our Family

We are so glad you found us. The Seventh-day Adventist Central Church in Visakhapatnam is a place where people of every age and background come together to encounter God, build genuine friendships, and serve our city.

Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or looking for a church home, there is a place for you here. Come as you are and experience the hope we have in Jesus Christ.
