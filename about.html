Our Story

Rooted in Faith, Growing in Grace

The Seventh-day Adventist Central Church in Visakhapatnam has been a beacon of hope in our city for many years. What began as a small group of believers gathering to worship on the Sabbath has grown into a vibrant congregation of families, youth, and children united in Christ.

We are part of the worldwide Seventh-day Adventist Church, a global family of more than 22 million members who share a common faith, mission, and hope in the soon return of Jesus Christ.

Our doors are open to everyone. We invite you to worship with us, study the Word of God together, and join in serving our community.

🎯 Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ who live as His loving witnesses and proclaim to all people the everlasting gospel of the Three Angels' Messages in preparation for His soon return.

👁 Our Vision

In harmony with the Bible and the promises of God, our vision is a community transformed by the love of Christ, ready to meet our Lord when He comes again.

What We Believe

Our Core Beliefs

As Seventh-day Adventists we hold the Bible as our only creed and cherish these fundamental teachings drawn from Scripture.

  • The Holy Scriptures — The Bible is the inspired Word of God and our final authority.
  • The Trinity — One God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • Salvation in Christ — Saved by grace through faith in Jesus.
  • The Sabbath — The seventh day is a holy day of rest and worship.
  • Baptism — By immersion, a public expression of faith in Christ.
  • The Second Coming — The blessed hope of Christ's soon return.
  • Healthful Living — Our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit.
  • The Great Controversy — The cosmic conflict between Christ and Satan.
